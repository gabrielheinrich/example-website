01 / THE COMPOSER

FINDING THE HUMAN
IN EVERY FRAME.

I’m Elias Vale. I write music for the things a picture can’t quite say.

Based in London, I compose original scores for independent film, documentary and television. My work lives somewhere between the concert hall and the recording studio — bringing felt piano, live strings and imperfect electronics into the same room.

Every collaboration begins with listening. To the story, to the silence, and to what a film is asking us to feel.
